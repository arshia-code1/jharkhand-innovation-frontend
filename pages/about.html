About the Platform

Connecting Government, Citizens, Universities and Industries to solve real-world societal challenges.

Our Purpose

Creating a collaborative ecosystem for innovative and practical problem solving.

For Citizens

Citizens can report real problems and challenges faced by their communities.

For Government

Government departments can identify, verify and manage important societal challenges.

For Universities

Universities can contribute research, ideas, expertise and student teams.

For Industries

Industries can provide technology, expertise and implementation support.

Our Vision

To build a digitally connected ecosystem where societal problems can be transformed into innovative and practical solutions.

Our Mission

To connect citizens, government departments, universities and industries through a transparent and collaborative digital platform.

Our Approach

Identify problems, verify challenges, invite solutions, create partnerships and support implementation.

Societal Challenge Platform

A Government of Jharkhand initiative connecting citizens, government, universities and industries.
