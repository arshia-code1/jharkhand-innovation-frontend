Healthcare Open

Improving Rural Healthcare Access

📍 District: Ranchi, Jharkhand

🏛 Government Department: Department of Health, Medical Education & Family Welfare

Problem Description

Rural communities often face difficulties in accessing quality healthcare services due to limited medical facilities, shortage of healthcare professionals and geographical barriers.


Expected Outcome

Develop an innovative and practical solution that can improve access to healthcare services for people living in rural areas.


Current Situation

Rural communities currently face limited access to healthcare facilities, medical professionals and essential healthcare services.


Problem Priority
