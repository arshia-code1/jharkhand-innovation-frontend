How It Works

From identifying a problem to implementing a collaborative solution.

01. Report a Problem

Citizens can submit real societal problems faced by their communities.

02. Government Verification

Government officials review and verify submitted problems.

03. Public Challenge

Verified problems become challenges for universities and industries.

04. Submit Solutions

Universities and industries propose innovative solutions.

05. Collaboration

Government selects suitable partners for collaborative problem solving.

06. Implementation

Partners work together to develop and implement the solution.
